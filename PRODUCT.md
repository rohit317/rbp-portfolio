# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers skimming a candidate's portfolio to decide whether to reach out for interviews.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Rohit Totlani, a full stack developer. It presents who he is, his projects, and a way to make contact. Success = a recruiter understands the profile in seconds on the first screen and can act (contact/links).

## Positioning

A hands-on full stack developer presented through a living, animated personal site (typewriter hero, WebGL shader backdrop, particles) — the site itself doubles as evidence of front-end craft.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind v4 + React; fonts via next/font (Geist, Geist Mono, Fraunces).
- Owner-committed design elements: WebGL shader background (PageBackdrop/ShaderFlow), TextType typewriter hero, particles, floating pill nav. Do not remove.
- Hero typed headline must never wrap to two lines at any viewport width.
- Dev server runs on port 3001 (port 3000 is occupied by another app).
- Placeholder portrait in use (`/josh.webp`); real portrait exists untracked at `public/rohit.webp` — swap pending owner confirmation.

## Evidence on Hand

Real portrait image (public/rohit.webp, untracked). No testimonials, press, or metrics — none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. First screen communicates "full stack developer" instantly.
2. Motion and atmosphere support, never block, reading.
3. Honest content only; placeholders flagged, not faked.
